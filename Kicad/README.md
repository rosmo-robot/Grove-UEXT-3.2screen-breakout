# Grove stand for ESP32 TFT Touch with Camera (ILI9341), J1

Status: draft v3, generated by script. Not opened in KiCad. Checked in Python only: header-to-GPIO map against the Makerfabs V1.1 schematic, copper clearance >= 0.2 mm, copper to edge >= 0.3 mm, every net connected (GND via the B.Cu pour).

## Mechanical
- 38.1 x 71 mm, 2 layers. Width equals the 15-pin header (15 x 2.54 mm).
- Right-angle 15-pin male header on the north edge, legs pointing off the edge into J1. Board lies flat behind the upright main board.
- Pin 1 (3V3) is on the LEFT, viewed from above with the header edge at the top. Match the labels on J1 before powering.
- Ports in two columns, pin rows vertical: J2 to J4 down the left edge, J5 to J9 down the right edge.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| J2 LIGHT | IO36 | 3 | J5 I2C | IO27 SCL, IO26 SDA | 14, 15 |
| J3 POT | IO39 | 4 | J6 DHT11 | IO18 | 12 |

Right-hand order is fixed by routing: top to bottom must follow descending header position, or signals cross on one layer. J5 to J9 are renumbered from v1 (BUTTON was J5); the schematic refs are updated to match.

## UEXT (J10, placed, NOT routed)
2x5 male header, bottom of the board, pin 1 square (Olimex UEXT rev B host pinout). Pads already carry nets, so KiCad shows a ratsnest. Route it yourself.

| UEXT pin | Signal | GPIO | J1 pin | Shared with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3V3 | n/a | 1 | all ports |
| 2 | GND | n/a | 2 | GND pour |
| 3 | TXD | IO22 | 7 | none (new net UEXT_TXD_IO22) |
| 4 | RXD | IO35 | 5 | none (new net UEXT_RXD_IO35) |
| 5 | SCL | IO27 | 14 | J5 I2C |
| 6 | SDA | IO26 | 15 | J5 I2C |
| 7 | MISO | IO19 | 11 | J7 BUZZER |
| 8 | MOSI | IO23 | 9 | J8 RELAY |
| 9 | SCK | IO18 | 12 | J6 DHT11 |
| 10 | SSEL | IO5 | 13 | none (new net UEXT_SSEL_IO5) |

- Use UEXT or the shared Grove ports, not both at once.
- IO35 is input-only (fine for RXD). IO5 is a strapping pin.
- Silkscreen format: signal, GPIO, #J1 pin, Grove port. Pins 5,7,13 of J1 now carry nets (were NC).

## Electrical
- All ports 3V3 only. J1 has no 5 V.
- SIG2 is not connected except on I2C. IO21 is not connected. IO35, IO22, IO5 go to UEXT only.
- Signals on F.Cu. 3V3 on B.Cu (bus and two vertical spines). GND is a B.Cu pour.
- C1 10 uF and C2 100 nF sit near the header. I2C pull-ups are already on the main board.
- Unplug the camera FPC: the same GPIOs drive the camera bus.

## Check before ordering
- Zone: press B in KiCad to refill GND.
- Footprints are written inline with library names. Confirm the right-angle header leg length reaches into J1.
- Connectors are JST PH 4-pin 2.00 mm. Not verified to mate with your Grove cables.
- Reference and value text is hidden on the footprints. Port names are board silkscreen.
- Not verified: Grove relay and RGB stick at 3.3 V.
